**Audience**: KuizKu batch `batch_secondary_01_sk_bm_t1`

This document reclassifies each of the 29 blueprint candidate slots into A / B / C confidence tiers for the **first** batch of 25 items. Items not in A (or selected B) are deferred to a later batch after teacher verification.

---

## Re-classification philosophy

- **A — HIGH CONFIDENCE**: T1 KSSM scope is well-established, T1 textbooks (where they exist outside Reference DB) commonly cover this, and the risk of mistargeting is low. These items can be generated for Batch 01 without teacher review of the underlying topic.
- **B — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE**: Likely T1 scope but not 100% confirmed. Use sparingly in Batch 01 (≤ 5 of 25). Items should be authored conservatively so even if the topic is slightly too advanced, the wording still tests a T1 student.
- **C — LOW CONFIDENCE / INFERRED**: Topic may be beyond T1 OR vocabulary/level is uncertain. **Excluded from Batch 01.** Will be revisited in a later batch after authoritative KSSM T1 syllabus is consulted.

For Batch 01, if a slot falls into C, the blueprint prefers **more A items** over padding with C items. Difficulty can be modulated by varying question style (recognition vs comprehension vs application) within A topics.

## Special review notes (per user request)

The following topics were re-examined explicitly:

1. **Kata kerja transitif vs tak transitif** → T1 KSSM does introduce basic kata kerja categories, but the explicit "transitif vs tak transitif" classification with required object-presence test is typically covered more rigorously at T2/T3. **Moved to B** (use conservatively — frame as simple object-vs-no-object examples).

2. **Imbuhan akhiran** → Imbuhan endings (`-an`, `-kan`, `-i`) ARE in T1 scope, but the full 3-suffix productive system is more rigorously tested at T2. **Kept as A but limited to 1–2 examples** (basic `-kan` / `-i` only, not deep coverage of all three).

3. **Imbuhan apitan** → Apitan (`peN-…-an`, `peN-…-i`, `ke-…-an`) is typically introduced at T2 or T3, not T1. **Moved to C** (excluded from Batch 01).

4. **Ekspositori (Petikan)** → T1 Pemahaman is predominantly naratif + deskriptif. Ekspositori is more T2+. **Moved to C** (excluded from Batch 01).

5. **Vocabulary scope** → No T1-specific word list exists; synonym/antonym scope is extrapolated. **Some items moved to B** with very common T1-level vocabulary only (basic daily words, school items, family terms).

6. **Pilihan diksi / register** → Diksi register sensitivity is more rigorously tested at T3+. At T1 it's mostly limited to formal vs informal greeting style. **Moved to B**.

7. **Kesantunan bahasa (register)** → Similar to #6. T1 covers basic register (use `cikgu` not `awak`), but explicit kesantunan modules are T2+. **Moved to C** for the explicit register item, **kept in B** for basic honorific usage.
